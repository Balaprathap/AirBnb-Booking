# 🏠 Airbnb NYC 2019 - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores the **Airbnb NYC 2019 dataset**, which includes approximately 49,000 listings and 16 columns of both categorical and numerical data. The analysis focuses on uncovering trends and insights related to **listing availability**, **pricing**, **host activity**, **reviews**, and **neighborhood dynamics**.

Airbnb is a major player in the global travel and accommodation sector, and data-driven decisions are essential for enhancing customer satisfaction, optimizing host strategies, and supporting the platform’s growth.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify which features most influence listing performance.
- Understand pricing trends based on location and room type.
- Visualize listing distributions and availability across NYC boroughs.
- Analyze reviews and guest engagement metrics.
- Provide actionable business recommendations based on data.

---

## 🗂 Dataset Description

- **File:** `AB_NYC_2019__.csv`
- **Size:** ~49,000 rows, 16 columns
- **Features include:**
  - `id`, `name`, `host_id`, `neighbourhood_group`, `neighbourhood`, `latitude`, `longitude`
  - `room_type`, `price`, `minimum_nights`, `number_of_reviews`, `reviews_per_month`
  - `calculated_host_listings_count`, `availability_365`

---

## 📊 Key Techniques Used

- Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Descriptive Statistics and Grouping
- Correlation and Pairwise Analysis
- Text Vectorization (BoW and TF-IDF)
- Geospatial Mapping with Latitude/Longitude
- Feature Importance (Random Forest)
- Visualizations (Seaborn, Matplotlib)

---

## 📈 Business Insights

- **Reviews per month** and **number of reviews** are the strongest predictors of listing success.
- Entire homes/apts are the most expensive and in highest demand.
- Neighborhoods like Manhattan and Brooklyn dominate listings and activity.
- Shared rooms are rare and contribute minimally to platform engagement.

---

## 🧠 Business Impact

The insights can be used to:
- Guide pricing strategy and promotional campaigns.
- Recommend listing improvements for hosts.
- Allocate marketing budgets to high-performing neighborhoods.
- Enhance platform recommendations using feature importance.
